Project Overview

The Campus Lost and Found Platform provides a centralized and searchable place for lost-and-found information on campus.

Lost-item notices are currently scattered across different communication channels and may quickly become buried by newer messages. Our platform aims to reduce repeated searching and provide a clear process from reporting an item to successful collection.

Main Features

Report Items

Post and update lost or found notices with consistent item information.

Search & Filter

Search notices by item type, date, location and current status.

Match Suggestions

Receive ranked suggestions for potentially matching lost and found items.

Private Claims

Submit ownership evidence privately and communicate during the claim process.

Collection Tracking

Track claim decisions and confirm successful return of an item.

Moderator Review

Handle inappropriate notices, duplicates and disputed claims.

Case Workflow

Open → Claim under review → Awaiting collection → Returned

Rejected or withdrawn claims leave the item available for other possible matches.
