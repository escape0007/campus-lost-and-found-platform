01 / Project overview

Less searching.
A clearer next step.

Notices scattered across communication channels are easy to miss. The proposed platform brings consistent item records, search and claim progress into one browser-based service for students and assigned moderators.

This website presents delivery progress and the project plan. The local Java application now supports accounts, notices, search, calibrated suggestions, alerts, private claims, independent return confirmation and assigned moderation. Original source deliveries remain archived. This information site does not accept notices or run the backend.

02 / First-release scope

One complete path through a case.

Planned capabilities
F1

Report & record

Create, update and archive Lost/Found notices with consistent fields, stable links and change history.

F2

Discover & receive alerts

Filter notices, inspect ranked suggestions and choose new-match alerts or manual checking. Suggestions do not prove ownership.

F3

Make a private claim

Submit identifying evidence and discuss it in a private case conversation with the notice author.

F4

Decide & confirm return

Allow one approved claim per item and linked loss. Both handover parties independently confirm a successful return.

F5

Report & review

Report inappropriate or duplicate notices and disputes. Case access is limited to the claimant, notice author and assigned moderator.

Outside the first release: image recognition, a native mobile app, payments, delivery, live location tracking and direct WeChat integration.

Planned case lifecycle

  1. Open
  2. Claim under review
  3. Awaiting collection
  4. Returned

A rejected or withdrawn claim reopens the item only when no other pending, held or approved claims remain. Cancelling an approved claim requires assigned-moderator review.

Closed is not Returned. Archiving does not confirm a physical return. A dispute raised before completion blocks completion; a later report preserves the recorded Returned outcome.
